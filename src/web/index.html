How to play

You are a mote of light in a dark that is learning you.

Collect every mote, then reach the Gate to descend. There are five depths.

The Readers are not monsters. They are models. Each one watches a different pattern in your movement and, before you move, marks the tile it believes you will step onto. Step there and you lose Composure.

  • ↑↓←→ / WASD move
  • Space wait (waiting is also a habit)
  • E Exhale — spend Nerve for Breath
  • Q then a direction — Decoy, plant a lie
  • H show your Tells — the map of your own habits
  • Esc menu

The crux: the fastest route is a repeated route, and repetition is exactly what a Reader learns. When a Reader is certain about you and you go somewhere else, it is startled — and a startle is the only real source of Breath. You cannot outrun the dark. You have to lie to it.
